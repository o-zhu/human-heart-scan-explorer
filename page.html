INSIDE THE EXPLORER

A heart, in another dimension.

This independent explorer reconstructs donor hearts from the open Human Organ Atlas. The image comes from a static scan of a preserved donor heart. The full-resolution source is 19.85 µm; the local whole-organ views are reduced for interactive use. Ultra cutaways use compact 79.4 µm display textures; their compression changes each grayscale value by at most one level out of 255. The selected section has first priority while the complete compressed section collection prepares in the background. The global download bar shows progress and lets you pause or resume. Clean Scan reversibly hides detached fragments; anatomy colors show approximate chamber and vessel regions. Surface finish softens shading ridges while retaining the measured cut-face pixels.

Echo Windows refine each in-scan view from measured source data. Completed standard-view sections can be saved on this device for faster repeat visits; adjusted planes continue to sample the finer volume. An initial volume preview remains labeled at its actual resolution until fine data is ready. Real ultrasound movies are independent reference studies matched by named view; they are not registered to this donor. TTE Lab is an interactive, CT-derived ultrasound simulation. The isolated heart contains no measured cardiac motion or complete chest anatomy, and its acoustic properties and clinical probe positions have not been validated.

Navigate the Volume

Rotate & zoom

Drag the heart with one finger to orbit it. Drag with two fingers to pan, and pinch to zoom. With a mouse, drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, and Shift-drag or right-drag to pan. Arrow keys rotate the focused view; + and − zoom. Double-click to reset. Use the view buttons for a fixed perspective, or reset to return to the starting view.

Look inside

Choose a clipping plane and move its position to reveal the interior. Flip changes which side is retained. Slice Brightness and cut-face contrast adjust the measured scan texture independently of surface lighting. Detailed cut-face images load on demand and retain the documented scan intensities.

Adjust the image

Threshold controls which intensities are visible. In Volume mode, opacity changes how much of the tissue you can see through. The colors are illustrative, not measured tissue colors. Surface, volume and maximum intensity offer different ways to inspect the scan.

Browse sections

Scroll over any section image, swipe on a touch screen, or use its slider and step buttons. Enlarge gives one section the whole section panel. Fullscreen keeps the heart and CT Sections together. Coronal and sagittal sit above the full-width axial section. Click a section or its title to align the 3D cutaway to that plane. Scrolling or moving its slider moves the section and 3D cut together. Click within the image to position linked crosshairs; Shift-scroll magnifies that section. Alt-scroll advances five sections. Section names follow each source’s orientation. For LADAF, coronal, sagittal and axial are approximate specimen planes; other donors retain neutral plane names until reviewed. Camera presets are not verified anatomical directions.

Ultrasound Exploration

Compare Echo Windows

Select TTE or TEE, then browse the grouped views or search by name. Reference movies use the local cine controls or the source website’s embedded player, with links to the original study and source information. Choose 3D or CT slice for the left comparison pane. The ultrasound occupies an equally sized right pane. Use 3D alignment to compare the ultrasound orientation or an upright anatomy view. Standard views reuse their exact saved sections when available; adjusted planes refine from the finer volume. Surrounding reference anatomy stays clear of the heart; the intact skeleton has a fixed illustrative fit. Model details and view guidance are available below the comparison. Views beyond the isolated scan retain their reference media and illustrative probe context.

Move a probe

Open TTE Lab and drag the heart to orbit it. Chest Orientation shows the selected whole heart inside fixed reference chest anatomy. Orbit that view independently, or choose Place Probe to click, tap or drag to any visible skin surface. Starting views sit above it, with keyboard controls below. The keyboard works immediately: W/S and A/D slide along the chest skin, Q/E rotate, I/K tilt, and J/L fan; Shift gives fine control. In free-position mode, R/F move out of plane. For optional mouse steering, choose Control the Probe; Escape releases that captured cursor. Sliders and draggable controls remain available in Probe Settings.

Steer a TEE probe

TEE Lab has independent insertion, shaft rotation, tip flexion, side flexion and multiplane controls. Hold W/S to advance or withdraw, A/D to turn, Q/E to change the electronic angle, I/K to flex the tip, and J/L to flex sideways. Keyboard controls work immediately throughout the active lab; Shift gives fine control. The electronic angle rotates the scan plane within the tip. Use the four starting views, switch between simulated ultrasound and measured CT, and lock the probe to hold a pose. The tract and insertion scale are illustrative and are not registered to a measured esophagus. The instrument and both images follow the same accepted pose.
