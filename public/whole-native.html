The original intensities are filtered in three dimensions with total variation denoising (weight 800, 16 fixed iterations), then combined with the coarse guide near its tissue boundaries. Native detail now adds a reversible cleanup of small, compact, detached tissue fragments. It preserves retained surface positions, protects elongated or flat fragments and keeps every component that crosses the region boundary. The original reconstruction and removed fragments remain available in Settings.

19.85 µm is the source sample spacing. It does not establish anatomical accuracy. Denoising and the guide can change thin structures, small cavities and connections. The whole-heart overview is a coarser reference with a separate experimental overlap repair; the right panel applies tissue-component cleanup to the selected native region. These surfaces are intended for anatomical exploration.

Noise Cleanup Comparison · New

In 13 sampled native blocks, the conservative cleanup suppressed 662 compact interior components and retained all 221 components touching block boundaries. It removed 15,570 of 11,942,037 foreground samples (0.130%) and 68,604 of 4,242,633 surface triangles (1.617%). These selected regions do not establish whole-heart anatomical accuracy.

The test compared compact-component filtering, stronger total variation, Gaussian filtering, morphological opening and intensity hysteresis. Component filtering selects complete foreground components, keeping every retained triangle at its original position. It does not delete a vessel or cavity wall merely because that wall is a disconnected surface shell. Elongated or flat fragments are protected using their three-dimensional shape; curved or detached real tissue can still meet the removal criteria.

The separate overview repair cancels exactly coincident triangles with opposite winding, then removes same-orientation duplicates. It does not infer tissue from triangle size. It can reveal gaps where earlier simplification collapsed thin surfaces, so the original display remains available.

Measured Denoising Comparison

Across 32 sampled native cores, the selected filter reduced extracted triangles from 2,301,576 to 570,575, approximately fourfold. In a separate cleft-rich example, it retained more narrow low-density structures than a Gaussian filter at 1.5 voxels. These tests compare image-derived surfaces; they do not provide anatomical ground truth.

Each sampled core had real neighboring source data. Fixed iterations and a 16-voxel halo produced bit-identical shared planes in the tested adjacent blocks. The five-bit stored edge crossings add at most 0.3102 µm of numerical movement relative to the processed field; that bound excludes filtering and anatomical uncertainty.
